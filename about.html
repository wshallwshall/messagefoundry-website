Our story

We built the interface engine we wished we had

MessageFoundry started in May 2026 to give healthcare teams an open-source alternative to costly interface engines. We wanted interfaces teams could read, test, and maintain themselves.

The project is in Early Access and developing quickly. Help test it in a sandbox and tell us what needs work.

Working with interfaces

Interfaces your team can maintain

Run the engine in your environment. Keep your interface logic in your own repository.

  • Run the open-source engine without per-interface license fees.
  • Use AI assistance to draft migration code, then review and test it before cutover.
  • Configure interfaces with guided wizards or write them in Python.
  • AI assistance is optional. The engine runs without a model.
Why we made it open

Why the engine is open source

MessageFoundry, LLC builds MessageFoundry and releases it under the GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL). The source is available to read, run, and modify under its terms.

We want healthcare teams to spend less on interface licenses and keep more control over the systems they depend on.
